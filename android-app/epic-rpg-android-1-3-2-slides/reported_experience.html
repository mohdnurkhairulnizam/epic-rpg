Reported Experience

The reported experience was sound without a visible alert

When a treasure timer ended while the phone display was off, the alarm could be heard, yet the expected notification was not visibly available until the screen woke.

Expected reward moment
Sound + visible prompt
A family sees the timer result and can re-enter the child flow immediately.
Reported gap
Sound without visible prompt
A time-sensitive reward cue can be missed while the screen remains locked.

Release goal: Make the local treasure alert eligible for Android lock-screen presentation while retaining the existing timer sound and return-to-app flow.

Illustrative Android lock-screen notification context. Device makers and user privacy settings can still affect final presentation.
